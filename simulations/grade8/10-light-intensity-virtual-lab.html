🔬 Virtual Lab: Effect of Light Intensity on Photosynthesis

Biology · Grade 8

1 · The research question

Does a plant make food faster when it gets more light?

What we know

Green plants make their own food (glucose) by photosynthesis. It happens in the chloroplasts, which contain green chlorophyll. Chlorophyll absorbs light energy.

carbon dioxide + water —light, chlorophyll→ glucose + oxygen

Oxygen is a product. A water plant (pondweed, Elodea) releases oxygen as small bubbles.
👉 More bubbles per minute = faster photosynthesis.

How do we change light intensity?

We move a lamp closer to or further from the plant. The closer the lamp, the brighter the light on the plant (higher light intensity).

Short distance → bright light. Long distance → dim light.

✍️ Your hypothesis — make a prediction

What will happen to the number of oxygen bubbles when we move the lamp closer to the pondweed?

There is no wrong answer here — scientists test their predictions. You will check yours in Step 5.

📋 Method

  1. Fill a boiling tube with water and add a little sodium hydrogencarbonate.
  2. Put a fresh piece of pondweed in the tube, cut end upwards.
  3. Stand the tube in a beaker of water (water bath) and switch off the room lights.
  4. Place the lamp 10 cm from the plant. Wait 1 minute so the plant can adjust.
  5. Count the bubbles for 1 minute. Repeat 3 times.
  6. Repeat at 20, 30, 40 and 50 cm. Calculate the mean for each distance.

⚠️ Safety: the lamp gets hot — do not touch the bulb. Keep water away from the plug and cable.

🧩 Fair test — sort the variables

Independent = what we change   Dependent = what we measure   Controlled = what we keep the same

5 · Analyse and conclude

🔍 Why does light matter?

Light gives the energy that chlorophyll uses to make glucose. More light energy → more glucose made each minute → more oxygen released → more bubbles. But at very bright light the rate stops increasing — now something else (like carbon dioxide or temperature) is in short supply. We call this a limiting factor.
