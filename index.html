About me

I studied Biology at Hacettepe University in Ankara, graduating as an honour student, and then worked as a molecular biologist at VFV Laboratory in Skopje, running Real-Time PCR diagnostics for SARS-CoV-2 and HPV through the pandemic. I am now completing an MSc in Phytochemistry & Natural Products at Mother Teresa University.

Since 2021 I have taught Biology at Maarif International Schools, where I now lead the Science Department and prepare my gymnasium class for the State Matura. In class I care less about memorising isolated facts and more about helping students observe, question, connect evidence and think for themselves — which is why I have built more than 20 interactive simulations for my lessons.

Outside the classroom I translate books, make Biology videos and posts as @biobesir, and run — I was national U19 champion in the 800 m and 1500 m.
