About me

I studied molecular biology at Hacettepe University in Ankara, then led the molecular biology department of a diagnostic laboratory in Skopje, running real-time PCR for SARS-CoV-2, HPV and chlamydia.

Since 2021 I have taught Biology at Maarif International Schools. In class I care less about memorising isolated facts and more about helping students observe, question, connect evidence and think for themselves. My students have taken first place in national Biology competitions.

Outside the classroom I write, translate, read, and run — I competed for the national athletics team.

    Uncontaminated samples from asteroid Bennu hold ammonia and amino acids, some unknown in Earth’s biology. They do not prove life came from space, but they show its ingredients may be common across the solar system.

    Understanding stress: a survival mechanism and a health risk
